Finishing Equipment

Power Float & Poker Vibrator

Power Float

A power float is a machine used to smooth and finish concrete surfaces. It consists of a rotating disc or plate that floats on the concrete surface, removing imperfections and creating a smooth finish.

Poker (Concrete Vibrator)

A poker, also known as a concrete vibrator, is a tool used to consolidate and eliminate air pockets in freshly poured concrete. It consists of a vibrating shaft inserted into concrete to remove air bubbles and ensure a dense, uniform finish.
