V
Power Float
A power ﬂoat is a machine used to smooth and ﬁnish concrete surfaces.
It consists of a rotating disc or plate that ﬂoats on the concrete surface,
removing imperfections and creating a smooth ﬁnish.
Poker (Concrete Vibrator/Poker Vibrator)
A poker, also known as a concrete vibrator or poker vibrator, is a tool used to 
consolidate and eliminate air pockets in freshly poured concrete. It consists of a 
vibrating shaft (poker) that is inserted into the concrete to remove air bubbles
and ensure a dense, uniform ﬁnish.
